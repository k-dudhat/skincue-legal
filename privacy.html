Privacy Policy

Last updated July 17, 2026

This Privacy Notice for SkinCue ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we access, collect, store, use, and share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Download and use our mobile application (SkinCue), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice

Users take photos of their face from three angles (front, left, right), which are analyzed by AI to assess visible skin characteristics such as acne, hydration, pore visibility, and pigmentation. Based on the analysis and information you provide (skin type, concerns, goals), the app generates a customized skincare routine, tracks daily adherence, and monitors progress over time. SkinCue is intended for general wellness and cosmetic skincare guidance. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

1. What information do we collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

In short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register on the Services, use the skin analysis feature, participate in activities on the Services, or contact us. This includes:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Photographs of your face submitted for skin analysis (up to three per scan: front, left, and right angles)
  • Profile photographs, if you choose to add one
  • Self-reported skin information, including your skin type, skin concerns, skincare goals, current routine, date of birth, gender, and how consistent your routine is
  • Routine adherence records, meaning which routine steps you mark as completed and when

Sensitive information

With your consent, or as otherwise permitted by applicable law, we process the following categories of sensitive information:

  • Biometric data. Photographs of your face are submitted by you for the purpose of skin analysis. Depending on your jurisdiction, facial images may be treated as biometric or sensitive personal information. We use them only to generate your skin analysis and to let you compare your scans over time. We do not use them for facial recognition, identity verification, or to identify you across services.

Social media login data

We provide you with the option to register using your existing Google or Apple account. If you choose to register in this way, we will collect certain profile information about you from that provider, as described in How do we handle your social logins.

Application data

If you use our application, we may also collect the following information if you choose to provide access or permission:

  • Camera access. The app requests camera access so you can take the photos used for skin analysis. We access your camera only while you are actively taking a scan.
  • Push notifications. We may request to send you push notifications about your account or certain features, such as routine reminders. You can turn these off in your device settings at any time.
  • Diagnostic and crash data. We use Sentry, a third-party error monitoring service, to collect crash reports and technical error information. This helps us find and fix problems in the app.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

As part of our Services, we offer products, features, or tools powered by artificial intelligence, machine learning, or similar technologies (collectively, "AI Products"). The terms in this Privacy Notice govern your use of the AI Products within our Services.

Use of AI technologies

We provide the AI Products through third-party service providers ("AI Service Providers"), specifically OpenAI.   • Image analysis of user-submitted facial photographs
  • Generation of personalized skincare routine recommendations

How we process your data using AI

All personal information processed using our AI Products is handled in line with this Privacy Notice and our agreements with third parties.

Limitations of the AI analysis

SkinCue provides cosmetic skincare guidance. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, and it cannot detect skin cancer or other medical conditions. Automated analysis can be inaccurate and can miss things a clinician would identify. You should not rely on SkinCue to assess whether a skin concern is medically significant. If you are worried about a mole, a spot that is changing, or anything that will not heal, see a qualified healthcare professional.

6. How long do we keep your information?

In short: We keep your information for as long as you have an account with us, and we automatically delete older scan photos.

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. No purpose in this notice will require us to keep your personal information for longer than the period of time in which you have an account with us.

Specifically:

  • Scan photographs. We retain the photos from your first scan (your baseline) and from your ten most recent scans. Photos from older scans are automatically deleted from our storage.
  • Analysis results. The results of your scans — scores, notes, and routine recommendations — are retained for the life of your account, including for scans whose photos have been deleted, so that your progress history remains intact.
  • Account information. Your name, email address, and settings are retained for the life of your account.

You can delete individual data and your entire account at any time from within the app. When you delete your account, we delete your scan photographs, analysis results, profile photo, and account record. When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will either delete or anonymize it, or, if this is not possible (for example, because it has been stored in backup archives), we will securely store your personal information and isolate it from any further processing until deletion is possible.

9. Controls for do-not-track features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.
