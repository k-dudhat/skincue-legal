2. Description of Service

SkinCue is a mobile application that uses artificial intelligence to analyze photos of users' skin and provide personalized skincare routine recommendations. Users can take photos of their face from three angles (front, left, right) which are analyzed by AI to assess skin conditions such as acne, hydration, pore visibility, and pigmentation. Based on the analysis and user-provided information (skin type, concerns, goals), the App generates a customized skincare routine, tracks daily adherence, and monitors progress over time.
