Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI analysis work?

SkinCue uses computer vision AI to analyze photos of your face. It identifies common skin concerns like acne, dryness, and uneven texture, then generates a personalized routine based on what it sees.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your photos and skin data are private and used only to provide you with personalized recommendations. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Open the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently removed.

Why don't I get a complete routine?

Try completing onboarding and providing skin info. The more we know, the better the routine.
